Research interests
======
To advance Computer Vision and Biometrics research that translates into deployable technology, applying strong analytical skills and international academic and applied research experience to face recognition under real-world settings such as: low resolution, long range, cross-spectral acquisition, and limited data, as well as anti-spoofing, privacy, and decentralized machine learning, and to contribute to problems of high social and scientific impact through research, collaboration, teaching, and the supervision of new researchers.

Research projects
======
* [CarMen](https://carmen-horizon.eu/) - Non-stop Biometric Border Control for Secure and Efficient Crossing (EU Horizon Europe) - Idiap, 2024 - 2026
  * Face data collection (RGB, NIR, through-glass, on-the-move) and research on cross-spectral face recognition for vehicular border control.
* [PopEye](https://popeye-project.eu/) - On-the-move biometric identification at EU external borders (EU Horizon Europe) - Idiap, 2024 - 2026
  * Long-distance face and gait data collection and research on long-range face and gait recognition.
* [AIM-GATE](https://www.frontex.europa.eu/innovation/eu-research/research-grants/project-aim-gate-LrBF4H) (Frontex Research Grants) - Idiap, 2025 - 2026
  * Data collection of challenging border-crossing scenarios, carried out together with PopEye and CarMen.
* [SENTINEL-UK](https://www.idiap.ch/en/scientific-research/projects/SENTINEL-UK) - Securing Age Estimation and Digital IDs against Presentation and Injection Threats (Innosuisse) - Idiap, 2024 - 2025
  * Detection of presentation and digital injection attacks for age verification systems.
* [SOTERIA](https://www.soteria-h2020.eu/) - User-centric privacy and decentralized personal data vault (EU H2020) - Inria, 2023 - 2024
  * Privacy on decentralized machine learning and Trusted Execution Environments for data-centric scenarios.
