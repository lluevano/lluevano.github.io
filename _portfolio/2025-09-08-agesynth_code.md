This is the code repository release for the paper "Identity-Preserving Aging and De-Aging of Faces in the StyleGAN Latent Space" accepted at the IEEE International Joint Conference on Biometrics (IJCB) 2025. It implements the aging/de-aging latent direction modeling, the feature selection approaches, and the generation of the AgeSynth dataset of synthetic faces at different ages.
