Presentation of my PhD research on affordable real-time very low resolution face recognition for video-surveillance applications, as a PhD student at Tecnológico de Monterrey, Campus State of Mexico. The consortium gathers doctoral students from all Tecnológico de Monterrey campuses and is usually held in Monterrey; this edition was held online due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
