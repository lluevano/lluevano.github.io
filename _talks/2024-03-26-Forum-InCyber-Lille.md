Talk on behalf of the EU H2020 [SOTERIA](https://www.soteria-h2020.eu/) project at Forum InCyber (FIC) 2024 in Lille. SOTERIA developed a user-centric tool for European citizens to manage their personal data in a decentralized, secure data vault. The talk presented our work at Inria on bringing machine learning to the data vault while preserving privacy, using decentralized learning and Trusted Execution Environments.
