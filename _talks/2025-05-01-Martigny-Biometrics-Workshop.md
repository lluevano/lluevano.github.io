Poster and live demo at the 2025 Martigny Biometrics Workshop, organized with the European Association for Biometrics (EAB) and CITeR. The demo showed our method for aging and de-aging faces by editing the StyleGAN2 latent space while keeping the identity of the person, ahead of its publication at IJCB 2025.
